Every unplanned stop costs a plant hours. What if an AI could test — maintenance plans before you approve one?

AI proposes. The simulator verifies. The operator decides.

The problem

Fixed rules are expensive to change

Plant managers choose fixed rules for maintenance and dispatch. Changing them on the real line is expensive, and a wrong change stops the plant.

— of orders arrive on time with the fixed rule.

The solution

Propose, verify, then a person decides

  1. 01 The model proposes

    It writes maintenance and dispatch policies as validated JSON.

  2. 02 The twin scores them

    A SimPy digital twin runs each policy across thousands of scenarios.

  3. 03 The operator decides

    One card comes back, with Approve and Reject.
